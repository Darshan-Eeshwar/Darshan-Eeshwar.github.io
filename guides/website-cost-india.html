What does a business website cost in India?

Published 17 September 20266 min read

Website cost depends less on the number of screens and more on the business work the site must perform. A clear brochure site, a catalogue and a transaction-heavy store have different design, content, integration and support needs.

Indicative starting ranges

For planning purposes, a focused starter presence may begin around ₹15,000, a multi-page growth website around ₹30,000, while commerce and custom workflows require a tailored quote. These are indicative figures, not universal market prices.

What changes the cost?

  • Number and complexity of pages
  • Original copywriting, photography or illustration
  • Product catalogues, payments and external integrations
  • Multiple languages and accessibility requirements
  • SEO research, analytics and automation
  • Ongoing maintenance and content updates

Costs beyond development

Plan separately for a domain, hosting, business email, premium software, payment-gateway charges and advertising. Confirm ownership and renewal responsibility before approving a proposal.

Good comparison rule: compare written scope, ownership, support, timeline and exclusions—not only the final price.

Questions to ask before accepting a quote

  1. What exact pages and features are included?
  2. Who supplies and approves the content?
  3. Are domain and hosting registered in the client’s name?
  4. What revisions and post-launch support are included?
  5. What third-party charges will recur?
