Samsung Health connection & tracking limits

In Samsung Health, open Settings → Health Connect and allow sharing. In the Android companion, tap Import Samsung Health, choose permissions and allow the selected data to be saved to this fitness account. Imports read the last 30 days while the app is open.

Phone steps start when tracking starts. Carry your phone; a visible notification stays on. Android battery settings, force-stop and reboot can interrupt tracking. Open the app and restart when needed. Samsung totals can update later as Samsung Health shares data. This is a test APK; physical-phone testing is still needed.

Automatic steps use the higher available phone or Health Connect count for each date, rather than adding both. Multiple phones use the highest daily count. Manual step entries add extra steps. Calorie values require a manual record or Health Connect data and are never inferred from steps.

Account & privacy

Signed in as

Daily records and goals are stored in your private backend account and load when you sign in on another device. The save indicator confirms when changes reach the server.

The Android companion can sync automatic steps and Samsung Health activity to this account. The browser displays synced data and supports manual logs. Sleep, water and food remain manual in this version. Phone distance is estimated unless imported from Health Connect. JSON backups include the manual journal and routines; automatic activity remains in the backend.

Export a backup before clearing or restoring. Backups contain your fitness records; keep them private. The journal is not a medical device. Dimple Enterprises home
