Automatic steps & privacy

Darshan Eeshwar Fitness uses your phone’s own step sensor. Counting is enabled by default when the app opens. Android asks for Physical activity permission; notifications show tracking status. No third-party health app is required.

Carry your phone. Pause in the app or notification at any time; your choice is remembered. Reopening the app resumes enabled tracking after permission approval. Android battery settings, force-stop and reboot can interrupt tracking. Open the app again if counting is interrupted. Steps before the initial sensor baseline are not backfilled.

Phone steps sync to your private account. Multiple phones use the highest daily count; manual entries add extra steps. Distance is estimated using your stride length. Calories and active time use your recorded values and workouts. Physical-phone testing is still required for this test APK.

Account & privacy

No login required.

Daily records and goals are stored in a private cloud journal linked to this device. The save indicator confirms when changes reach the server. Clearing this site’s browser data creates a new journal, so export backups regularly.

The Android app counts steps using your phone sensor and syncs them to this device journal. The browser displays synced data and supports manual logs. Fastrack readings can be entered in the Watch tab; compatible Bluetooth sensors can supply heart rate. Sleep, water and food are otherwise manual. Phone distance is estimated from steps and your stride length. JSON backups include the manual journal and routines; automatic activity remains in the backend.

Export a backup before clearing or restoring. Backups contain your fitness records; keep them private. The journal is not a medical device. Dimple Enterprises home
