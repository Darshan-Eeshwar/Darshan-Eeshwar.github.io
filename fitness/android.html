Your own fitness journal, powered by your phone’s step sensor. Automatic steps and private device sync, without another health app or login.

Download Android APK · v1.1

Android 10 or newer. Signed development/test APK; physical-phone testing is still required. If an older test APK cannot be updated because its signing key differs, sync pending steps before uninstalling it. Your cloud journal remains available on this device.

Steps start automatically

  1. Download and install the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the app once. It creates a private device journal automatically.
  3. Allow Physical activity when Android asks. Counting starts automatically. Allow notifications to see tracking status.
  4. Carry your phone. Steps save locally and sync to your private device journal about once a minute when online.

Your tracking, your choice

Use Pause automatic steps in the app or Pause in the notification at any time. Pausing is remembered on this phone. Tap Resume automatic steps to turn it back on.

Your daily momentum

Original green, amber and coral progress rings track steps, active time and activity calories. Explore monthly goals, hourly steps, estimated distance, workout routines, sets and reps, water, food, sleep, body measurements and your private cloud journal.

Tracking and privacy

No third-party health integration or health-data read permissions. Phone distance is estimated from your stride length. Active time and calories use your recorded values and workouts; they are not automatically inferred from step counts. No GPS, camera, microphone or contacts access is requested. Session tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore.

Counting begins at the first sensor baseline; earlier steps are not backfilled. Android battery settings, reboot, force-stop and process loss can interrupt tracking. Reopen the app to resume enabled tracking. Phone sensor batches are assigned to their delivery hour, so hourly bars are approximate. The website alone cannot count steps with the screen locked. This is a personal journal, not a medical device.
