Darshan Eeshwar Fitness
for Android

Automatic phone steps, account sync and Samsung Health import, connected to your existing fitness journal.

Download Android APK · about 11 MB

Android 10 or newer. This is a compiled, signed development/test APK, not a Play Store production release. Physical-phone testing is still required.

Start automatic steps

  1. Download and open the APK on your Android phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from the browser you used.
  2. Open Darshan Eeshwar Fitness and sign in with your existing username and password.
  3. Tap Start automatic steps and allow Physical activity. Allow notifications to see the tracking status.
  4. Carry your phone. Steps are stored locally and synced to your account about once a minute when the network is available. The notification includes a Stop action.

Import Samsung Health totals

  1. In Samsung Health, go to Settings → Health Connect and allow the activity data you want to share.
  2. In the Android companion, tap Import Samsung Health, choose permissions and approve saving the selected data to your fitness account.
  3. The foreground import reads available Samsung activity from the last 30 days. Samsung's sharing and synchronization can delay updates, and some displayed Samsung metrics may not be shared.

What is included

Daily progress hearts for steps, active time and activity calories; monthly totals and goals; hourly steps; recorded/imported total burned calories and distance; and your existing workout routines, journal, nutrition, sleep, recovery and body-measurement tools.

Calories are logged or imported and are not calculated from steps. Active time uses recorded workout minutes or daily/imported totals. Health Connect active time is derived from shared exercise sessions and may differ from Samsung's broader daily active-time figure.

Tracking and privacy

Phone counting begins when you start tracking and does not backfill earlier steps. Android battery settings, reboot, force-stop or process loss can interrupt it. Reopen and restart after those events. Sensor batches are assigned to their delivery hour, so phone hourly bars are approximate. The website alone cannot count steps with the screen locked.

No GPS, camera, microphone or contacts access is requested. Tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore. You choose the Health Connect data that can be read and uploaded. This is a personal journal, not a medical device. Future test APKs can have a different debug signing key; sync pending steps before uninstalling an older test build.
