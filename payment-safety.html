Verify before you pay.

This page helps customers validate a payment request. It is not a payment gateway and does not collect card, bank, UPI PIN or OTP information.

Before paying

  1. Match the project, invoice amount and milestone.
  2. Confirm the beneficiary or UPI details through an existing trusted conversation.
  3. If bank or UPI details changed, call the official number before paying.
  4. Keep the transaction reference and payment receipt.

Never share

  • OTP or UPI PIN
  • Banking password or card PIN
  • Screen-sharing or remote-control access
  • Identity documents unless formally required and securely requested
